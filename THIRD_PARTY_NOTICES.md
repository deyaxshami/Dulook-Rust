# Third-Party Notices

## hadith-api

`data/hadith-fallback.json` contains a selected and reformatted subset of the
Arabic and English Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim editions from
[fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api), pinned to
commit
[`df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784`](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api/commit/df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784).

The upstream edition metadata identifies Muhsin Khan for the English Bukhari
edition and Abdul Hamid Siddiqui for the English Muslim edition. The Arabic
edition authors are listed upstream as unknown. See the upstream
[`References.md`](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api/blob/df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784/References.md)
for its source bibliography.

file. Dulook's transformation pairs the two languages, selects records,
normalizes references, and reformats the source as JSON; it does not alter the
Hadith text.
